Privacy, Terms & Food Allergens

Privacy & Data Use

We collect only the name, phone number, delivery address, and email needed to fulfill your orders, track delivery, and apply loyalty rewards. Your cart and latest Order ID stay locally on your device. No payment card data is stored on our servers.

Account & Data Deletion

To request export or permanent deletion of your account and order history at any time, email contact@hungerbirds.rest or contact us on WhatsApp at 0325-7867774 or call 0325-7861114.

Terms of Ordering

Minimum delivery subtotal is Rs. 500; takeaway orders have no minimum. Delivery charges vary by distance and are confirmed prior to dispatch. Menu availability and prices sync live from our kitchen POS.

Food Safety & Allergen Notice

Our kitchen prepares items containing dairy, gluten/wheat, eggs, soy, sesame, and spices on shared equipment. If you have a food allergy or dietary requirement, call us at 0325-7867774 or 0325-7861114 before placing an order.
